# Everyday relationship readings

Eva should help someone understand their own situation in ordinary language. A romantic overview uses five sections, in this order:

1. The first person's possible thoughts, feelings, needs and effort.
2. The second person's possible thoughts, feelings, needs and effort.
3. What could be working well.
4. What is difficult, uncertain or mismatched.
5. Advice with a reason and one realistic thing to say or do.

Use supplied names and perspective. Do not assume the reader is a partner, assign genders, invent mutual attraction, or treat cards as access to private thoughts. Explain “energy” through communication, feelings and effort. Distinguish an observed behaviour from a possible explanation for it.

For example, if the user says they usually arrange dates, an appropriate direction might be: “You could ask, ‘Would you choose a day and plan our next meeting? I want to see what feels comfortable for both of us.’ Their response would give you more information than trying to guess from slow replies.” This is an illustration of practical wording, not a generated reading or a fact about any user.

Vietnamese should sound like a conversation: “Nếu bạn thường là người hẹn gặp, bạn có thể thử nói: ‘Lần tới bạn chọn ngày và lên kế hoạch nhé? Mình muốn biết cách nào thoải mái cho cả hai.’ Cách họ trả lời sẽ giúp bạn hiểu rõ hơn, thay vì phải tự đoán.” Prefer “mình” and “bạn”, clear reasons and familiar words over abstract energy jargon.

Retain every drawn card's real meaning and spread position. Explain how the named cards support the reflection within the five sections; do not add a second repetitive card-by-card answer. A single card is a shared lens, not two separate draws. With no cards, discuss only the situation supplied. Ask one useful question when essential context is missing.

A narrow follow-up answers the requested part. A continuation resumes the existing outline. A new subject overrides the earlier topic and selected focus. Other kinds of readings retain their card headings and situation-specific meaning, helpful possibility, difficulty, advice and concrete direction.

## Acceptance

- Test English and Vietnamese with the same supplied circumstances and actual cards.
- Check each person's side appears separately before positives, difficulties and advice.
- Check all card names/positions remain accurate and no unreported motives become facts.
- Check advice follows from the user's circumstances and respects their limits.
- Check a correction, a one-person follow-up, a topic change, and one/no-card cases.
- Automated prompt/provider tests verify instructions and context routing. Only actual generated samples can support an editorial assessment of the prose.
- Keep the existing AI model, output limits, credit rules and provider budget unchanged.
